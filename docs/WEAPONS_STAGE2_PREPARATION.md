## Official procedure to implement

1. **Power and arming (already occurs at turn start):** A weapon is operational only if powered, undestroyed and appropriately armed. A weapon fired or deactivated resets its arming cycle; cooldown turns may prohibit arming. Persist last-fired, last-deactivated, arming progress, cooldown, mount-specific critical effects and any weapon sub-gun damage. Plasma Accelerator offers variable 1/1, 1/2 or 1/3 turn firing intervals, with different damage values; the owner selects the legally available output based on uninterrupted arming. Twin Array consists of two individual guns, each able to fire once per turn, potentially in different roles.
2. **Complete movement and pre-fire segments:** Positions, headings, EW allocations and all fire-related movement penalties are frozen from the authoritative state. Resolve relevant combat pivots, close-combat EW, ramming and weapon-induced movement first (or explicitly retain them as manual adjudication barriers).
3. **Secret fire determination:** Each player privately declares, per physical mount and per twin-array gun, offensive target or defensive reserve, mode, chosen Plasma Accelerator charge level, called shot if optional rule enabled, and ordered attacks against each target. Validate ownership, powered/armed status, arcs, LOS, target existence, rate of fire and restrictions. Do not expose the opponent's uncommitted declarations. Once both players lock, declarations cannot change.
4. **Reveal then defensive assignment:** Reveal all offensive declarations simultaneously. Only **after** seeing declared attacks, the defender assigns eligible predeclared defensive guns to specific incoming shots or elects not to fire. The defender can see the declared weapon mode/called shot, but **must decide before any to-hit or damage rolls**. Separate this reactive allocation barrier from the initial secret declaration; existing `fire_commit` and `fire_reveal` need a defensive-allocation substate or new barrier. Defensive fire resolves during the incoming shot's tier. Twin Array's two guns can split offensive/defensive roles. Apply intercept degradation for multiple guns against the same non-ballistic shot; no such degradation for ballistics. Lasers and weapons with intercept N/A cannot intercept.
5. **Resolution tiers:** (a) ballistic impacts, (b) ship weapons, (c) fighter vs fighter, (d) fighter drop-out, (e) fighter vs ships, (f) other weapon fire. A gun destroyed in an **earlier** tier cannot fire later; weapons declared in the **same** tier still fire simultaneously. The player chooses and commits the resolution order of their shots against a given target **before dice**. Initially only the ship-versus-ship tier is automated for two Vorchans; all other tiers remain explicit no-op/manual gates, not falsely resolved.
6. **To-hit for each attack:** Determine actual bearing and attack direction (forward 60°, port/starboard 120°, aft 60°). On an exact boundary, the **defender** chooses the receiving direction, and that choice is consistent for all fire from that source ship in the turn. Same-hex fire uses positions just before the last unit entered that hex. Check physical mount firing arc including half-hex boundaries and LOS (terrain/large objects may block; ordinary ships do not). Determine target's printed defence rating by facing; subtract defensive EW, add attacking ship's offensive EW, add the weapon's fire-control modifier for target size, subtract range penalty (ceil steps; double **penalty**, not distance, if no offensive EW lock-on), and apply only verified other modifiers (roll/pivot, jammer, terrain, shield/intercept, etc.). Roll d20 **at or below** the modified to-hit number. Return complete auditable modifier breakdown. No invented maximum range or natural-1/20 rule.
7. **Damage:** Use the **weapon's actual damage dice and mode** and the attack distance (separate from to-hit range). Plasma Accelerator is standard-mode plasma damage with distance falloff; **plasma halves system armour, dropping fractions**, for that hit only. Roll the correct Vorchan forward/aft hit chart d20; a `primary` result triggers a second d20 on the primary chart. For ambiguous hit tokens, let the defender choose a legal actual SCS component (correct section, bearing and operational status). Deduct applicable shields first, then non-ablative armour separately for each volley, mark verified damage boxes, transfer overkill to appropriate structure with its armour, and mark system destruction. If target structure is destroyed, follow same-tier simultaneity and post-fire structural collapse rules. Never silently select the first twin array/thruster or invent missing armour/boxes.
8. **After fire:** Mark weapon usage and next legal firing turn. Track systems damaged **this turn** for end-turn criticals; critical rolls and effects occur in `criticals`, not immediately at the hit. Resolve attached systems of destroyed structures in the appropriate end-turn step. Ensure power, EW, thruster and weapon operational state reflect persistent damage on following turns.

## Existing implementation inventory and blockers

- `shared/phases.mjs`: 23 phases, including `fire_commit`, `fire_reveal`, `fire_resolution`; currently manual placeholders. Fire resolution is a single phase and must have server-enforced internal substeps.
- `shared/fire-sequence.mjs`: simple declaration lock and four-tier grouping; lacks defensive allocation, fighter drop-out/other weapons and actual authoritative server validation. Extend without pretending the current helper is a firing engine.
- `shared/ew-combat.mjs`: verified offensive/defensive EW arithmetic and no-lock doubling; integrate with stored turn allocations rather than trusting client modifiers.
- `shared/damage-plan.mjs`: preliminary hit-location/mode planning, **does not apply SCS damage**. Extend or create an audited damage executor; never enable it as a complete engine.
- `shared/centauri-combat-data.mjs`: Vorchan forward/aft/primary d20 charts available. Hit tokens (e.g. `twin_array`, `retro_thrust`, `main_thrust`, `side_thrust`) do not uniquely identify actual mounts. Add defender-choice resolver and tests.
- `shared/vorchan-trial-library.mjs`: 1 Plasma Accelerator, 2 Twin Array **mounts** (up to 5 individual shots per turn: one plasma plus two guns on each twin array, subject to arming and eligibility); weapon damage, fire control, power and variable plasma ROF data available. `arc:null` on **all three** mounts, and `completeForAutomaticCombat:false`.
- `shared/vorchan-scs-identification.mjs`: verified weapon tracks but `forward_port_thrust` and `forward_starboard_thrust` still unverified; `completeness.allWeaponArcsIdentified:false`, and other SCS completeness flags false. Source-of-truth SCS transcription must be completed before auto damage.
- `shared/vorchan-power-economy.mjs`: per-mount power switching available; must add arming/deactivation/cooldown state transitions and validate any optional partial twin-gun power shutdown.
- `docs/vorchan-combat-preflight.md`: earlier preliminary plan; superseded by this phase-ordered integration plan for implementation sequencing. Reuse its verification gates.
- **Rules discrepancy check:** current `FIRE_TIERS` has four groups, whereas the detailed Appendix A additionally lists fighter drop-out and all other weapons fire. Represent the full sequence in the server state machine.
- **Source limitation:** The repo's trial record explicitly labels weapon arcs as unverified. Its earlier preflight document mentions forward 90° and 270° arcs, but those must be visually confirmed on the original Vorchan SCS; do not promote that assertion into authoritative mount geometry until checked.

## Recommended implementation work packages (ordered)

**W1 — Ship SCS and weapons data audit.** Obtain/inspect original Vorchan v3 2E/SF sheet. Verify each mount's exact arc geometry and facing, power, armour, damage boxes, defence values, plasma ROF/damage options, twin array shots, weapon range and all hit charts. Resolve incomplete forward thrusters and damage-diagram gaps. Deliver canonical per-mount records with evidence and a test asserting combat-ready status only if verified.

**W2 — Pure targeting and firing calculations.** Add `shared/weapon-targeting.mjs` (bearing, arc, LOS, range, direction choice, modifier breakdown, deterministic to-hit), `shared/weapon-readiness.mjs` (power, damage, arming, sub-guns, plasma charge), and unit tests. Use no unverified arcs or automatic hit shortcuts.

**W3 — Authoritative secret declaration and reactive defence.** Add D1 persistence for per-player declarations, mount/gun reservations, ordering and revisions. Worker endpoints for preview, commit, reveal and defensive assignments. Explicit two-player barriers and idempotency; prevent read access to opponent orders before reveal and prevent editing after lock.

**W4 — Deterministic server firing executor.** Add a persisted fire-resolution substep cursor and roll journal. Freeze declarations and state, resolve defence before attacks, preserve simultaneous ship-fire eligibility, use server RNG, prevent duplicate rolls on retries. Persist weapon usage and arming changes.

**W5 — SCS damage bridge.** Resolve hit charts and defender choices, apply shields/plasma/armour/overkill/structure, update exact SCS damage boxes and operational state. Pause for legitimate defender selections. Keep unverified tracks blocked. Integrate critical eligibility and end-turn state.

**W6 — User interface.** Interactive weapon mounts on SCS (power/ready/cooldown/damaged, per-gun Twin Array buttons), map-based target selection with valid arc overlays and LOS feedback, transparent to-hit previews, secret order ledger, lock/reveal indicator, defender intercept assignment, ordered shot-by-shot combat log, visible SCS damage and turn-to-turn rearming.

**W7 — Automated and live multiplayer acceptance.** Unit tests for arcs, same-hex, edge ties, LOS, lock-on/no lock-on, EW, FC by target class, range/damage falloff, plasma charge levels, twin gun splitting, intercept degradation, exact hit-chart mapping, armour/overkill, same-tier simultaneous fire, earlier-tier destruction, critical timing, security and concurrent commits. Extend isolated two-account staging smoke to fire across two turns and confirm both clients see the same persisted result. Do not touch production or merge to main without approval.

## Minimum two-Vorchan playable acceptance

Both players finish movement; select individual SCS guns; see legal targets and computed modifiers; secretly lock orders; see both declarations only after both lock; assign eligible defensive Twin Array shots; server resolves the ship tier once with reproducible logs; both clients see consistent hits and real SCS box changes; weapon arming/cooldown and critical eligibility persist into turn 2. Unverified data must cause a clear blocked/manual adjudication state, **not fabricated combat**.

**Recommended first coding target:** W1 and W2, then W3. Do not expose a Fire button that claims to inflict damage until W5 is complete and tested.
