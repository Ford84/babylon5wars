# Babylon 5 Wars — Stage 1 scope

Rules sources: Second Edition Rules Compendium (primary) and Kitchen Sink Appendix A (supplemental phase list). The 23 phase entries in shared/phases.mjs are a workflow scaffold, not complete rules enforcement.

The prototype provides account access, persistent games, invitations, ships, a hex-map preview, order commitment and phase progression. It does not yet implement validated ship movement, combat, damage, EW calculations, ship-control sheets, conditional participant eligibility or full fog-of-war. Secret orders are revealed after the commitment barrier.

Acceptance criteria: test two independent players, concurrent final submissions, private information handling, invitations, R2 images and turn wrap. Review rules against the official compendium before expanding the engine.
